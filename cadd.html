Our CADD Platform

An integrated, end-to-end pipeline from disease target identification to a validated therapeutic candidate.

Pipeline Deep Dive

The Complete CADD Pipeline

Every step is powered by integrated computational tools.

01

Disease Data Collection

Literature review, clinical databases and omics data integration to establish the biological context.

02

Target Identification

PPI networks, gene ontology enrichment and pathway analysis to find druggable targets.

03

Network Pharmacology

Compound–target–disease networks to elucidate multi-target mechanisms and hub nodes.

04

AI Virtual Screening

Deep learning screens millions of compounds from ChEMBL, ZINC and PubChem.

05

Molecular Docking

AutoDock Vina and Glide to evaluate binding modes, affinities and interactions.

06

MD Simulation

GROMACS and AMBER simulations validate binding stability over time.

07

Binding Energy & ADMET

MM-GBSA/PBSA free energy with ADMET profiling for pharmacological assessment.

08

Therapeutic Candidate

Validated lead compound with a full computational dossier and publication support.

What We Offer

Computational Services

End-to-end computational drug discovery for academic researchers, biotech startups and pharmaceutical companies.

Our Services

Comprehensive Computational Services

01

Computational Drug Design

Full CADD pipeline from target identification to lead optimization using structure- and ligand-based approaches.

02

Molecular Docking Studies

AutoDock Vina, Glide and GOLD to evaluate binding affinity and interaction modes of hit compounds.

03

Molecular Dynamics Simulation

GROMACS and AMBER simulation of protein–ligand complexes: binding stability, conformational change and thermodynamics.

04

Network Pharmacology

Drug–target–disease network analysis to elucidate multi-target mechanisms and synergistic combinations.

05

AI-Driven Virtual Screening

ML-accelerated screening of ZINC, ChEMBL and PubChem libraries for rapid hit identification.

06

Bioinformatics & Genomics

RNA-seq analysis, variant annotation, pathway enrichment and multi-omics integration.

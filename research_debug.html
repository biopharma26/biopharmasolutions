Methodology

Our Research Methodology

🎯
Target Identification

Systematic identification of disease-relevant molecular targets using network biology and AI-driven analysis of multi-omics datasets.

🕸️
Systems Biology

Network pharmacology approaches to understand disease mechanisms, drug-target interactions, and polypharmacological effects.

💊
Lead Optimization

Structure-activity relationship analysis, scaffold hopping, and bioisosteric replacement to optimize lead compounds for efficacy.

📊
Validation & Analysis

Rigorous computational validation through binding energy calculations, ADMET profiling, and molecular dynamics stability analysis.

🤖
AI Integration

Machine learning models trained on curated bioactivity data for accelerated virtual screening and property prediction.

📝
Publication & Dissemination

Research findings published in peer-reviewed journals with open collaboration for knowledge sharing and scientific advancement.
